# 05_Visualization

Scientific visualization and structural inspection tools for Molecular Dynamics simulations and molecular modeling workflows.

This module contains VMD-based utilities designed to facilitate structural interpretation, validate computational analyses, inspect molecular geometries, and generate visual representations of membrane systems and molecular orientations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Support interpretation of Molecular Dynamics results
- Validate geometric and orientational analyses
- Improve structural inspection workflows
- Facilitate visual communication of simulation results
- Generate custom molecular representations

---

## 📂 Contents

### draw_membrane_planes.tcl

Draws membrane boundary planes at user-defined Z coordinates.

**Applications**
- Membrane visualization
- Leaflet identification
- Ligand insertion analysis
- Orientation studies

---

### visualize_molecular_vectors.tcl

Displays molecular normal vectors and reference axes directly in VMD.

**Applications**
- Orientation validation
- Tilt-angle verification
- Molecular ordering studies
- Structural inspection

---

### interactive_geometry.tcl

Interactive improper dihedral calculator using atom picking within VMD.

**Applications**
- Geometric analysis
- Conformational inspection
- Structural validation
- Interactive molecular exploration

---

### filter_ligands.tcl

Creates customized systems by removing selected ligand molecules or residues.

**Applications**
- Concentration adjustment
- System preparation
- Comparative simulations
- Model refinement
